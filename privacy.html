Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

comfy-poster is a personal tool used by a single person (its owner) to post their own videos to their own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

What data it uses

  • Sign-in tokens that the owner grants to their own accounts on each platform, used only to upload and publish the owner's videos and to check upload status.
  • Basic account details returned by each platform at sign-in (such as the account or channel name and ID), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • The owner's own videos and captions, which are uploaded to the platform the owner chose.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Tokens are encrypted with the operating system's per-user encryption. comfy-poster has no server, does not send data to anyone other than the platform being posted to, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Other people's data

comfy-poster does not access, collect or store data about anyone other than its owner.

Deleting data

Revoking comfy-poster's access in a platform's account settings, or deleting its local data folder, removes its access and its stored data.
